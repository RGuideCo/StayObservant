<div class="hundred-intro">
  <p class="hundred-intro__label">The assignment</p>
  <p class="hundred-intro__statement">The mark needed to introduce the studio without taking over the work it made.</p>
  <p class="hundred-intro__context">54 Hundred brought video, photography, graphic design, and web work together under one Miami studio name. I developed an identity that could give those different services a common point of recognition while leaving room for each project to speak in its own voice.</p>
</div>

## Start with the name

I looked at how other visual languages could suggest a studio with a home in Miami, then began setting 5400 in very different typefaces. Some read like technology. Others felt closer to an editorial title. Seeing them together made the tradeoff clearer: the studio needed character, but the name still had to be read at a glance.

## Let 54 lead

The next round pulled the name apart. I tried 5400, 54 Hundred Media, and more compact arrangements before bringing the expressive 54 into a frame with Hundred beneath it. The number became a shorthand people could recognize quickly, while the word kept the full name present.

## Build in flexibility

The mark begins in black and white. Blush, pale blue, and coral give it other ways to meet photography without redrawing the logo. The Miami image studies provided a sense of place, but the identity did not have to depend on one particular picture to feel like 54 Hundred.

## Test it at a distance

The outdoor mockup asks for the opposite of a business card. It has to register from across a street. I kept the mark contained inside a simple white field and let the Miami beach image provide the setting. The exercise showed how a small, disciplined signature could still hold its place at public scale.

<figure class="hundred-feature">
  <img src="/optimized/Projects/54Hundred/billboard.webp" alt="54 Hundred billboard concept above a Miami street with the framed mark over beach photography" width="1800" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
  <figcaption>Billboard mockup used to test the identity at a distance.</figcaption>
</figure>

<p class="hundred-closing">The same mark could introduce someone at arm's length and hold its place on a city street. That was the measure of the system.</p>
