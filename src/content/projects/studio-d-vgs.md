<div class="structure-intro">
  <div>
    <p class="structure-eyebrow">The assignment</p>
    <p class="structure-statement">One system should be able to take more than one shape.</p>
    <p>At Studio D, I developed three directions for a modular retail display system for VGS. The work tested how a common set of parts could support graphics and merchandise in different spaces without demanding a new display for every use.</p>
  </div>

VGS needed a system that could change with the space, the merchandise, and the campaign while remaining practical to make and use.</p></div></div>
