## A city you can taste

For Estrella Damm's Tapas Journey in Brickell and Downtown Miami, I brought the neighborhood and the food together in one illustration. Familiar city forms become a setting for the tapas, while the bottle anchors the composition and keeps the offer easy to find.

![Estrella Damm Tapas Journey campaign illustration](/optimized/Projects/EstrellaDamm/tapas-journey.webp)

## Built to move through the city

The illustration was developed for more than one format. It appears in concepts for table talkers, bus shelters, and a trolley wrap, carrying the same idea from the restaurant to the street.
