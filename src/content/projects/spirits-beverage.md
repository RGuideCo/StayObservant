## Heineken

For Heineken, the design problem was physical presence. This bar concept turns the brand's Formula 1 partnership into a large, legible gesture while keeping the counter itself open and usable.

![Heineken Formula 1 bar arch concept](/optimized/Projects/SpiritsBeverage/heineken-bar.webp)

## Rémy Martin

The Rémy Martin serving ritual considers the moment a drink is presented. Materials, placement, and the form of the tray give the objects a deliberate order without making the experience feel overly formal.

![Rémy Martin serving ritual display concept](/optimized/Projects/SpiritsBeverage/remy-serving-ritual.webp)

## Rutte

For Rutte, the advertisement brings attention to the distinctive Celery Gin. The bottle stays central while the ingredient becomes part of the atmosphere around it.

![Rutte Celery Gin advertising artwork](/optimized/Projects/SpiritsBeverage/rutte-ad.webp)
